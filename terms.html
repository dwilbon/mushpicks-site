What the app is

MushPicks logs bets you already placed somewhere else and prices a hypothetical parlay from published odds. It does not accept wagers, hold funds, pay winners, or send a bet to a sportsbook. Play of the Day is a model comparison against the no-vig market. It is not a promise of profit and not advice from a professional handicapper.

You must be 21 or older. If gambling is causing problems, call 1-800-GAMBLER.
