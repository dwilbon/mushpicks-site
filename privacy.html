Privacy Policy

MushPicks is a bet tracker and parlay calculator. It does not take wagers, hold a balance, or pay winners. This page says what the app collects, who receives it, and how to delete it.

What we collect

  • Account email, display name, handle, the state you pick, unit size, and an optional monthly goal and daily stop.
  • Bets you log: sportsbook, selections, odds, stake, and the result once a game is graded.
  • Bet slip images you choose to upload. Those images are sent to OpenAI so the slip can be read. MushPicks does not keep the photo after that request.
  • A push token, if you allow notifications, so a graded ticket can notify this phone.
  • Purchase status from Apple, read through RevenueCat. We do not receive your full card number.

Who receives it

  • Supabase stores the account and the tickets so grading can run while the app is closed.
  • OpenAI processes a slip image only to turn it into text. The request uses their API under our key.
  • RevenueCat reads the App Store subscription so the app knows whether Pro or Elite is active.
  • The Odds API receives the sport, market, and sportsbook names when the board asks for prices. That request does not include your name, email, or stake.
  • Apple processes the subscription payment.

What we do not do

We do not sell personal data. We do not share it with data brokers. We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites. We do not use the slip photo for advertising.

How long it stays

Account data and logged bets stay until you delete the account or we delete them at your request. A slip image is not stored by MushPicks after the read finishes. OpenAI may retain API inputs for a limited time under its own policy.

Delete your account

In the app, open Profile and tap Delete account, then confirm. That deletes the login and the tickets stored for grading. Bets saved only on the phone are cleared too. Deleting the account does not cancel an App Store subscription. Cancel that in Settings, Apple ID, Subscriptions, at least 24 hours before a renewal.

You can also email the support address on the support page and ask for deletion. We will delete the account if we can match the email.

Children

MushPicks is for people 21 and older. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 21.
