How we find the edge

  1. 01

    We set our own price.

    For every game, our model works out how likely each side is from each team's last five games: points scored, points allowed, and the margin between them. Player props use the player's own recent game logs.

  2. 02

    We take the vig out.

    We strip the built-in cut out of the prices at four or more sportsbooks and blend them into the real odds the market is implying.

  3. 03

    We find the gap.

    When our price beats the market's by 3 percentage points or more, that's an edge. The bigger the gap, the more units we suggest. On a day when nothing clears that bar, the play is marked lower conviction.

  4. 04

    We only play real prices.

    We skip anything priced worse than -170, so you're never laying big juice for small returns.
